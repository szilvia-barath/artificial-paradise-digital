# Screen reader test: NVDA on Windows

A script for me (Szilvia) to run by hand. It has not been run yet: the "you should hear" lines are what the markup should produce, worked out from the code, not heard. They were worked out again on 15 November 2026, after the changes of group a of the UI/UX proposal, and again the same day after group b (C3, C5, C8): steps 3.4 to 3.7 and 8.4 to 8.6 are new. Worked out again on 16 November 2026, after group c (B3, C6, C7): steps 3.8, 4.12, 4.13 and 7.6 to 7.9 are new, and 3.4, 7.5 and 8.6 now expect something else. Worked out again on 16 November 2026, after group d (A4, B5, B6, B7, B10, B16, B17, D11, F1, F2, F3): steps 1.11, 1.12, 3.9, 4.14 to 4.17, 5.10, 5.11, 6.7, 8.7 and 8.8 are new, and 1.9, 3.3, 4.11, 5.5, 7.7 and 8.2 now expect something else. Worked out again on 16 November 2026, after groups e and f (D2, D8, E2, and step 1 of H1): step 7.10 (a page at 400% zoom) is new, and 9.5 names the floor plan's dot as blue, its new colour. D2 and D8 change only what is seen on a touch screen, and H1 only colours and weights, so no other step changes. Where NVDA says something else, write it in the "Heard" column and send the table back.

## Before you start

- NVDA (current version), speech on, Speech viewer open (NVDA menu → Tools → Speech viewer) so you can copy what was said.
- Run the whole script twice: once in Firefox, once in Chrome. Note the browser at the top of your copy.
- Address: the preview of the `wip/a11y-pilot` branch, with `?lang=en` at the end. Run steps 1 to 9 again with `?lang=hu` if there is time.
- Keys in this script: **NVDA** is Insert (or Caps Lock). Browse mode and focus mode: NVDA plays a sound when it switches. Inside the 3D view NVDA should switch to focus mode by itself, because the view has the role "application".
- Exact wording varies a little between NVDA versions and between Firefox and Chrome ("clickable", "button" before or after the name). Only note differences that change the meaning, drop something, or say something twice.
- Use a wide window (a laptop at 100%) except in steps 1.8 to 1.11 and 7.9, which ask for a narrow one.
- For steps 8.2, 8.7 and 8.8: NVDA menu → Preferences → Settings → Speech, tick "Automatic language switching (when supported)", and use a synthesizer that has both a Hungarian and an English voice (eSpeak NG has both; with Windows OneCore both languages must be installed in Windows). Without that, NVDA reads the English titles with the Hungarian voice, and those steps cannot show the change.
- Step 4.17 is the only step that loads Google's video player (Google Drive). Every other step leaves "Full video with sound" alone.
